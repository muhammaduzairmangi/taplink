Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10-09-2026

This policy explains how Taplink handles information when you visit this website or contact us to place an order.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to share with us directly, such as your name, business name, phone number, email address and order details, typically when you message us on WhatsApp or submit our contact form.

Our website itself does not run a server-side database. The contact form on this site opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message on your own device rather than submitting data to our servers, unless a backend integration has been added separately.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and process orders
  • To customize your NFC or QR product with the details you provide (logo, brand colors, destination link, contact information)
  • To communicate with you about your order

How we share information

We do not sell your information. We may share order details with printing or manufacturing partners solely to produce your product.

WhatsApp communication

Messages sent through our WhatsApp links are handled through WhatsApp's own platform and are subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy in addition to this one.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete information you've shared with us by contacting us using the details on our Contact page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to m.uzairmangi@gmail.com or via WhatsApp.
