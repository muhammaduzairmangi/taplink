How it works

From your first message to a working product on your counter.

01

Choose your product

Browse our products, or tell us about your business on WhatsApp and we'll suggest one.

02

Send your business details

Share your logo, brand colors, business name and where you want each tap or scan to lead.

03

We customize & program it

We design your product and program the NFC chip and QR code to open your chosen destination.

04

Tap, scan & connect

Your product arrives ready to use. Place it where customers will notice it, and it works immediately.

NFC is programmed. QR is printed.

Each NFC chip is individually programmed with your destination before it ships — that's what makes the tap work. The QR code printed alongside it points to the same destination, so it works as a backup for any phone.

Depending on the product, some setups can be reprogrammed later if your destination changes. Ask us about this if it matters for your business.
